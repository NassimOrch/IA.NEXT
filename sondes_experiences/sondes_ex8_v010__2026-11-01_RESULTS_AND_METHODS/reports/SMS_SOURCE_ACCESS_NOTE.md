# SMS source-access note — v010

The v010 SMS work uses the v009 train/validation split and the v009 **validation** preprocessor (fit on train only). The model pipeline materialized only the 3,102 train rows and 775 validation rows. It did not materialize train/validation/adaptation/test arrays for the full corpus, did not fit or select models on adaptation or test, and did not calculate adaptation/test performance.

There is an important qualification: reconstructing the deduplicated row indices from the raw SMS collection required streaming the raw corpus file (5,574 source lines) and computing text hashes to recover the first-occurrence unique-text order (5,171 messages). During that source-integrity pass, labels in all raw source lines were parsed to validate the `ham`/`spam` line format, including lines belonging to the previously defined adaptation and test partitions. Those hidden-partition records were not retained as features/labels, and their labels were not passed to fitting, early stopping, model selection, or metric computation. However, the source-file stream means it would be inaccurate to say the raw test/adaptation records were never encountered at all.

Moreover, this SMS corpus/split has documented historical exposure in earlier EX5/V007/V009 work. Therefore, v010 SMS is classified as **second-domain replication with known historical exposure**, not as a pristine external holdout or confirmatory test. The test and adaptation partitions remain unused for v010 fitting and evaluation. Any future strictly blind confirmation requires a new, previously unseen corpus and a pre-registered split whose raw contents are not present in the working inputs.
